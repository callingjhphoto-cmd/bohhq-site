No. 02

Who does what.

Software does the reading and the sums. A person at Back of House checks them. You decide.

  1. 1our softwareReads

    Your POS, accounts, rota, invoices and bank, linked once or sent as downloads. It reads; it never changes anything in them.

  2. 2our softwareFinds

    Does the sums and lists every leak in pounds, biggest first, with the working shown so you can check it.

  3. 3a person at Back of HouseChecks

    Looks over what was found, walks you through it, writes your weekly note and holds a monthly review with you.

  4. 4youSign

    You, or a manager you name, sign, say no or ask why. Nothing that moves money, pay or people happens until someone signs.

Then we fix it with your team: menu and costs, rota and sickness cover, training. The software never sends a message, posts, or changes your systems on its own.
