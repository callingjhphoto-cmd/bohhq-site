No. 02

What changes for you.

Six things an owner notices. The full capability list is for the call.

1More of what you take stays yours

Card fees, supplier prices, brand money you are owed, stock that walks, dishes priced under your margin: found in your own records and put in pounds, so you fix the biggest first.

2No surprises at month end

Every night’s takings checked against card and cash. Every invoice checked against what was delivered and what the supplier’s own copy says. Payments you did not approve, flagged.

3A rota that fits the night

Hours against the covers you will actually do, labour against the target you set, and cover found when someone calls in sick. Pay worked out for you to approve; your payroll provider still files it.

4One message a morning

Only what needs you, and you reply yes or no. During service, nothing reaches you unless it cannot wait. Licence dates, food-safety checks and council rules handled before they become a problem.

5Guests who come back, and quiet nights filled

Your own booking page and a brief before service: who is in, what they like, what went wrong last time. Reviews answered in drafts you approve. Offers for quiet hours, and a straight answer on whether each one made money.

6Fewer tools, one screen

Bookings, guests, staff, social and your website on the same records as your POS and accounts. For a one-site hospitality venue, 18 to 20 separate tools add up to about £1,641 a month at list prices; this does those jobs in one place.

SAMPLE: built and tested on invented venues, not yet live. Tool prices are published UK list prices read 18 Oct 2026, for a one-site hospitality venue of sample size; named tools are examples, not partners.
